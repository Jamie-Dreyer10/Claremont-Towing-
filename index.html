Supercar and exotic transport

The Lamborghinis already ride with us

Supercars and exotics are one of our specialities, and we are proud of the cars we carry. A lowered car does not belong on a hook. Our hydraulic flatbeds tilt to a shallow angle, so the front splitter clears the bed and the wheels never touch the road. From a breakdown in Constantia to a service bay in the city centre, your car arrives the way it left.
